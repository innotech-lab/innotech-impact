# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Decision-makers at startups, SMEs and institutions who need a dependable digital product or a technology partner.
- Young people and emerging innovators looking for accessible STEM learning and practical digital skills.

## Product Purpose

Innotech Impact presents a technology startup that develops software and technology solutions while delivering STEM education. The site must turn qualified interest into a project conversation or a training enquiry.

## Positioning

Innotech Impact connects the software it builds for organisations with practical STEM learning for the next generation of innovators in Burundi.

## Operating Context

Visitors evaluate services, examples of work, training opportunities, company mission and ways to contact the team. The application includes a React public site and a Django API with contact, quote and newsletter endpoints.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing React/Vite public site with routes for home, about, services, projects, training, contact and quotes.
- Django REST API exposes content and form endpoints under `/api/`.
- The existing Innotech Impact logo asset is preserved.
- Product claims, outcomes, client names and statistics are not verified and must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Innotech Impact.
- French is the primary product language.
- The supplied brief names software development, technology solutions and STEM education as core offers.

## Evidence on Hand

- Local logo and project imagery under `src/assets/`.
- Existing project names: Gnosis Platform, Kithub, ComLB, LM Dashboard and Women Tech. Their factual status and outcomes still need verification before publishing as case studies.
- No verified customer testimonials, metrics, prices or programme calendar are available in the repository.

## Product Principles

1. Make the two offers — software delivery and STEM learning — intelligible within seconds.
2. Use specific, honest evidence; never imply proof that is not available.
3. Give visitors a direct next step for a project, training or partnership.
4. Make the experience accessible on lower-bandwidth mobile connections.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public site should target WCAG 2.2 AA, use French-first plain language, preserve keyboard access and respect reduced-motion preferences.
